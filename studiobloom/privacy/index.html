StudioBloom Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 20, 2026

This policy applies specifically to StudioBloom: AI Photo & Video, provided by LLC “Amam Capital” under the Hollybit brand.

AI processing requires your permission. StudioBloom does not send your content for AI processing until you accept the in-app disclosure. You can decline, but generation features and the app will remain unavailable.

1. Data we collect

Depending on the features you choose, StudioBloom processes:

  • Photos and images you select, including faces, appearance, identity-profile images, reference images, and product images.
  • Text prompts, negative prompts, selected presets, aspect ratios, and other generation settings.
  • Generated images and videos returned to StudioBloom.
  • Account and purchase data, including a StudioBloom user ID, credit balance, subscription entitlement, product identifier, and transaction identifier. We do not receive your full payment-card number.
  • Operational data, such as app version, request status, safety results, error information, and timestamps needed to provide and secure the service.

2. How data is collected and used

Content is collected only when you select or enter it in StudioBloom. We use it to perform the image or video generation or edit you request, preserve identity or clothing when requested, apply safety checks, return results, maintain generation history, prevent abuse, grant credits and entitlements, provide support, and comply with law.

Do not upload another person’s image or content unless you have their permission.

3. Who receives data

  • Supabase hosts StudioBloom’s backend, authentication, temporary media references, job records, credit balances, and generation history.
  • fal.ai (Features & Labels Inc.) receives the selected photos or videos, prompts, and generation settings needed to perform AI processing. Depending on the chosen feature, fal.ai may process the request with models from Google (Nano Banana or Veo), Kling AI, or PixVerse.
  • Adapty receives the StudioBloom user identifier and App Store purchase and entitlement information needed to display products, validate purchases, restore purchases, and synchronize premium access.
  • Apple processes App Store purchases and provides transaction information needed to deliver the purchased product.

These providers may process data in countries other than your own. We require service providers handling StudioBloom data to use it only to provide their contracted services and to maintain the same or equivalent level of privacy and security protection described in this policy, subject to applicable law.

4. Safety processing

Prompts and selected media are checked before generation, and generated media is checked before delivery. Requests that violate StudioBloom’s safety policy are blocked. Safety results may be retained with the job record to enforce limits, prevent repeated misuse, and issue credits or refunds correctly.

5. Retention

We retain account, transaction, credit, and generation records for as long as needed to provide StudioBloom, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Media is retained only as needed to process, deliver, and display requested results or history. Provider retention may also be governed by the provider’s service terms and our agreements with that provider.

6. Your choices and rights

You may decline AI processing before any content is sent. You may also request access, correction, export, restriction, or ask us to delete personal data where applicable. To request deletion or exercise a privacy right, email compliance@hollybit.app. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

7. Tracking, sale, and advertising

StudioBloom does not sell personal data and does not use your photos, prompts, or generated content for cross-app tracking or third-party advertising.

8. Children

StudioBloom is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal data.

9. Security and changes

We use technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect data, including authenticated generation requests, access controls, and safety checks. No system can guarantee absolute security. We may update this policy as StudioBloom or legal requirements change and will update the date above.

10. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: compliance@hollybit.app.

General support: support@hollybit.app.
